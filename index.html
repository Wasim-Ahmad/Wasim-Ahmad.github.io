I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA), Nanjing. My research sits where unmanned aerial vehicles meet wireless communication, with a particular focus on UAV channel modelling, the Internet of UAV Things, and the application of machine learning and deep learning to the networks themselves.

My work addresses how a radio link behaves when one end of it is flying, and how far that link can be pushed in terrain where ground infrastructure is thin or absent — agricultural land, open water, mountainous zones and dense urban environments. More recently I have worked on the integration of large language models into intent-based vehicular communication, and on deep learning for intrusion detection on UAV links. I am looking for postdoctoral positions and research collaboration.

Research Areas

UAV Channel Modelling

Air-to-ground propagation and path loss across agricultural, maritime, mountainous and urban environments, validated against measured link behaviour.

Internet of UAV Things

Collaborative cloud computing for heterogeneous UAV networks, with coverage and handover optimisation using harmony-search methods.

Learning for Wireless Networks

Meta-learning applied to path loss prediction, letting a model generalise to a new channel from very few measurements.

LLM-Integrated Communication

Intent-based vehicle-to-everything systems where a language model turns high-level intent into network policy under latency and energy constraints.

Network Security

Deep learning for intrusion detection on UAV links, and privacy-preserving IoT frameworks built on trigger-action platforms.

Geospatial Machine Learning

Landslide susceptibility mapping and terrain risk assessment, applying machine learning to environmental data in mountainous regions.
